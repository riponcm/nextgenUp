## First comment (post immediately after submitting)

Hi HN, author here.

NextGenUp is a free, open source AI upscaler with a simple GUI. It handles images (up to 8K), video (up to 4K), face restoration with GFPGAN, batch processing, and a before/after slider. Everything runs locally using Real-ESRGAN, either in the browser via WebGPU or on a bundled local server. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Desktop apps for macOS, Windows and Linux, with FFmpeg and models bundled. You can also run it from source and use it from any device on your network.

Limitations: the compact model loses to Topaz on extreme fine detail, Pro video mode is slow without WebGPU, and macOS builds are unnotarized (right-click, then Open). MIT licensed.

Feedback welcome, especially on model choices for a max-quality tier.
